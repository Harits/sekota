# Landing Page Implementation Requirements

This document specifies the requirements for the Sekota Landing Page implementation, derived from the Figma design.

## 🎨 Visual Identity
- **Primary Font**: Montserrat (ExtraBold) for headings.
- **Secondary Font**: DM Sans for body and interactive elements.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Gradient from Green (#60BD65) to Cyan (#02B6CF).
  - Background: Clean white/off-white.
  - Dark Sections: Deep Navy/Black background for contrast.

## 🧱 Component Breakdown

### 1. Hero Section
- **Heading**: "Trusted Intelligence."
- **Visuals**: Large decorative blurs, floating UI card mockups.

### 2. Feature Grid ("Data Melimpah, Keputusan Masih Kabur")
- **Intro**: Tagline and subtext.
- **Cards**: 3 cards with icons, headings, and descriptions.
  - Data Tidak Terintegrasi
  - AI Tanpa Transparansi
  - ESD Hanya Beban Administrasi

### 3. Strategic Value Loop
- **Visual**: Circular process diagram with icons (Peta, Identitas, Alur, Validasi, Lapor).

### 4. Intelligence Suite
- **Heading**: "Intelligence Suite."
- **Cards**: 4 product cards (Veridia, Ascendio, Sociara, Ecoflow).
  - Each has a logo, description, feature list, and "Pelajari Selengkapnya" link.

### 5. E-Book & Panduan Strategis
- **Heading**: "E-Book & Panduan Strategis."
- **Cards**: 4 colorful cards (Blue, Orange, Green, Purple).
  - Each has a title, subtext, and "Lihat Detail" link.

### 6. Value Proposition (Dark Section)
- **Text**: "Sekota Bukan Sekadar Vendor, Bukan Sekadar Konsultan."
- **Cards**: 2 cards on the right with icons and text.
  - Kolaborasi Strategis
  - Solusi Berbasis Data
- **CTA**: "Hubungi Kami" button.

### 7. Contact Form ("Mulai Percakapan Strategis")
- **Info**: Contact details (Location, Email, Phone) on the left.
- **Form**: Input fields (Nama, Perusahaan, Pesan) and "Kirim Pesan" button on the right.

### 8. Global Footer
- **Layout**: 4 columns (Product, Perusahaan, Legal, Social).
- **Branding**: Logo and company description.
- **Bottom**: Copyright and language selector.
